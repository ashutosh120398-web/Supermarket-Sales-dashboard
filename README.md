# 🛒 Supermarket Sales Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** built to analyze supermarket sales, profit, and quantity across regions, product categories, and time periods.

## 📌 Project Overview

This dashboard provides a complete view of supermarket sales performance, helping identify top-performing states, seasonal sales trends, best-selling product sub-categories, and customer segment contribution to revenue.

## 🎯 Objective

- Track overall **Sales**, **Profit**, and **Quantity** sold
- Analyze sales trends **by month, quarter, and year**
- Compare performance **across states and regions**
- Understand sales split **by customer segment** (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)
- Identify top **product sub-categories** driving revenue
- Enable dynamic filtering by **Quarter, Year, State, and Month**

## 📊 Key Metrics (KPIs)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | 2.30M |

## 🧩 Dashboard Features / Visuals

- **Sum of Sales by Month Name** – bar chart showing monthly sales trend
- **Sum of Sales by State and Region** – US map view (Central, East, South, West)
- **Sum of Sales by Quarter** – pie chart breakdown (Q1–Q4)
- **Sum of Sales by State** – tree map of top-selling states (California, New York, Michigan, etc.)
- **Sales by Ship Mode** – trend across Standard, Second, and First Class shipping
- **Sales by Segment** – donut chart (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)
- **Sub-Category filter buttons** – Binders, Envelopes, Bookcases, Chairs, Furnishings, etc.
- **Detailed product-level table** – Product ID, Sales, Quantity, Profit, and Product Name
- Slicers for **Quarter, Year, State, and Month** for dynamic filtering

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Power BI Desktop** – data modeling, DAX measures, and dashboard design
- **Excel / CSV** – source data

## 📈 Key Insights

- Q3 contributed the highest share of sales (26.73%) among all quarters.
- California and New York are the top-performing states by sales.
- Consumer segment leads in overall sales contribution.
- Sales dip significantly during January and February compared to Q3/Q4 months.
